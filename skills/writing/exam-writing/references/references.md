## Known limits

- In a small class, the difficulty and discrimination indices have a large standard error (about 0.15 with 11 students). They help find miskeyed items and dead distractors, not compare items finely.
- The matching rules rest on general multiple-choice guidelines. No verified experimental research specific to matching was found.
- The advice to grade question by question is inferred from [12] and [13]. No verified study was found that compares it directly with grading exam by exam.
