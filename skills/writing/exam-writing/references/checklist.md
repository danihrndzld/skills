# Pre-print checklist

Answer each check with yes or no. A "no" tells you what to fix. The file in parentheses explains the rule.

## Exam plan

1. Is every item tagged with its cell in the table of specifications (objective and cognitive level)? ([table-of-specifications](table-of-specifications.md))
2. Do the points for each objective match the weight planned in the table? ([table-of-specifications](table-of-specifications.md))
3. Does the split of points across cognitive levels match the verbs in the course objectives? ([cognitive-level](cognitive-level.md), [table-of-specifications](table-of-specifications.md))

## Multiple choice

4. Can every stem be answered with the options covered? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md))
5. Are all stems positive, or does NOT/EXCEPT appear in bold capitals? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md))
6. Does every item have a single defensible answer, confirmed by someone else who answered it without the key? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md), [item-analysis](item-analysis.md))
7. Are the options of each item the same kind of thing and of similar length? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md))
8. Are the options free of "always", "never", "all of the above" and "none of the above"? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md))
9. Do all options agree grammatically with the stem (article, gender, number)? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md))
10. Is the key free of words copied from the stem? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md))
11. Does every distractor match an error you can name? ([options-and-distractors](options-and-distractors.md))
12. Are numeric options in order from smallest to largest? ([multiple-choice](multiple-choice.md))

## Short answer

13. Does every question state the verb, the scope ("one formula", "2 or 3 sentences") and the points for each part? ([constructed-response](constructed-response.md))
14. Is there a model answer written by the instructor within the space and time given? ([constructed-response](constructed-response.md))
15. Does the analytic rubric for each question list the acceptable alternative answers? ([constructed-response](constructed-response.md))

## Matching

16. Are the premises all the same kind of thing, and the responses too? ([matching](matching.md))
17. Are there more responses than premises, and are the extra ones plausible? ([matching](matching.md))
18. Do the instructions say what is matched with what and how many times each letter is used? ([matching](matching.md))
19. Do the premises and responses fit on the same page? ([matching](matching.md))

## Versions

20. Do all versions fill the same cells of the table with the same number of items and points? ([parallel-versions](parallel-versions.md), [table-of-specifications](table-of-specifications.md))
21. Is each letter (A to D) the key for a similar number of items within each version? ([parallel-versions](parallel-versions.md))
22. Do the clones change only surface data and keep the technique, the reasoning steps and the length? ([parallel-versions](parallel-versions.md))
23. Do items that share a scenario or a data set stay together after shuffling? ([parallel-versions](parallel-versions.md))

## Grading

24. Is the student's name in a place that can be covered while grading? ([constructed-response](constructed-response.md))
25. Has the key for every version been checked against the final printed exam, after the last reordering? ([item-analysis](item-analysis.md), [parallel-versions](parallel-versions.md))
